## Current Research Direction

### AI-Assisted TDD for Robotics Learning

I develop a browser-based learning environment that combines robotics programming, automated tests, hidden checks, simulation, behavioral telemetry, and guardrailed LLM feedback for self-guided practice.

**Built:** evaluation, telemetry, simulation, and tutoring workflows<br>
**Measures:** accuracy, efficiency, recovery, robustness, and learner experience<br>
**Status:** platform developed; experimental evaluation in progress

[Read the case study](./docs/ai-assisted-robotics-platform.md) · [Read the IEEE FIE paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/FIE61694.2024.10893417)

### Intelligent Tutoring and Behavioral Telemetry

My work studies how attempts, pass rates, time-to-recovery, edit-run cycles, stuck states, and learning outcomes can support personalized feedback. LLM workflows use prompt engineering and guardrails to explain errors and guide learners without disclosing complete solutions.

### Multimodal AI

I build systems that combine visual evidence, OCR, associated text, and transformer-based reasoning. The current digital-forensics pipeline emphasizes modality-aware routing, frozen labels, structured outputs, reproducible experiments, and auditable score fusion.

[Read the digital-forensics case study](./docs/multimodal-forensics-ai.md) · [Open the repository](https://github.com/CS-Ponkoj/Hate-and-Threat-Detection-in-Forensics)
